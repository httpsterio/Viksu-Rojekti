## Overview

In the epic view, cards whose status is in `doneStatuses` (roj-055) are treated separately from active cards. They remain part of the epic but are visually grouped and collapsed by default.

**Dependency:** roj-055 must be implemented first — this feature reads `doneStatuses` from config to determine which cards are considered done.

## Behaviour

Within each epic container, done cards are separated from active cards and placed at the bottom. They are hidden behind a disclosure button:

> **Show 4 done items**

Clicking expands the group in place. Clicking again collapses it. State is per-epic and not persisted — defaults to collapsed on load.

## Styling

Done cards in the expanded group render with reduced opacity and a muted border. No other structural changes — they still show title, ID, priority, tags, and are still clickable to open the modal.
